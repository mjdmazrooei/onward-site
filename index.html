How it works

  1. Set up once. Add your kids, your school days and your breaks. Then add each book: "Lessons 1 to 160", or paste the titles from the contents page.
  2. Open Today each morning. Each kid's lessons for the day, plus the subjects you teach together, shown once.
  3. When life happens, tap once. Unfinished lessons move forward and every finish date updates. A sick day is its own choice. Missing work never marks a day off for you.

Catch up in one tap

Earlier work shows in quiet grey, not red. Tap "Move unfinished work forward" and it lands on today in the same order. You see how each finish date moves before you confirm.

A record that stays true

Moving work forward never changes what you did on Monday. Every tap is saved with its date, undo is a new line, and you can export the whole history as a spreadsheet for your state or your online-school teacher.

Teach one subject to all of them

Plan a read-aloud once for three kids. When one is away, untick them before you save. Only the kids who were there get credit, and the absent one sees it as catch-up.

No account, works offline

Your plan lives on your phone. Each kid is a nickname, a color and a hero badge they pick. No photos, no birthdays. It works in the car, at the park and at co-op, with no signal.

Questions

When can I use it?

Onward is in private testing now. People who leave a deposit get an iPhone beta invite first, through Apple's TestFlight. Android comes after the iPhone version.

What happens to my deposit?

It comes off your first year when paid plans start. If you change your mind, reply to the receipt email and you get all US$5 back. No questions asked.

Does it work with my curriculum?

Yes, if your curriculum has numbered or listed lessons. You type how many lessons there are, or paste the titles. Onward is independent and isn't linked to any publisher.

Does it track attendance or hours?

Not in the first version. You get an exact, dated history of every lesson done, which you can export. Attendance and hours come later. Onward never tells you that you meet your local rules. Please check those yourself.

Where is my data?

On your phone. The app has no account and sends your plan nowhere. This website keeps only what you type into the forms. See the privacy page.
