Privacy

Last updated November 17, 2026

The app

The Onward app has no account. Your plan, your kids' nicknames and your history stay on your phone. The app does not send them to us or to anyone else.

This website

When you visit this site, we save a random visitor ID kept in your browser, which version of the page you saw, the link or ad you came from (if any), and which buttons you tapped. We use this to count how many people visit and how many are interested. We don't use advertising cookies, and we don't sell or share this data.

If you join the waitlist, we also save your email address, the number of kids you chose (if you chose one), and the time you agreed to be emailed. We use your email only to tell you when the beta opens.

If you leave a deposit, Stripe handles the payment. We see your name, your email and the amount. We never see your card number. Stripe's own privacy policy covers what Stripe keeps.

Where it is kept

Website data is stored with Supabase in Canada (Montreal region). Deposits are processed by Stripe.

How long we keep it

We delete waitlist emails and visit records within 12 months, or sooner if you ask. Deposit records are kept as long as tax law requires.

Your choices

Email hello@onwardschoolday.com to see, correct or delete your data, to leave the waitlist, or to get your deposit back. We'll reply within 30 days.
